# Video Game Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard exploring video game sales across gaming consoles, genres, regions, and developers. The project uses historical sales data to identify industry trends, compare regional preferences, and examine the commercial performance of games and publishers/developers.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze video game sales across platforms and regions.
- Identify the best-selling games and gaming consoles.
- Compare genre preferences in Japan, North America, and PAL regions.
- Examine historical video game sales trends.
- Identify leading developers based on total sales.
- Present findings through an interactive, multi-page dashboard.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI** — dashboard development, visualizations, and report navigation
- **Power Query** — data preparation, if used in the project
- **Video Game Sales Dataset** — historical game sales, platform, genre, publisher, and regional sales information

## Key Insights

The dashboard highlights several patterns in the historical data:

- **Console performance:** The PlayStation 2 leads the displayed console sales comparison, followed by the Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3.
- **Regional preferences:** Miscellaneous and Action are the top two genres in the displayed Japan, North America, and PAL comparisons.
- **Global hits:** Grand Theft Auto V ranks first in the displayed global best-selling games chart.
- **Developer performance:** EA Canada leads the displayed top-ten developer sales comparison.
- **Historical trends:** The time-series charts show sales activity rising through the 1990s and 2000s, followed by a decline in the later years represented in the dataset.

These observations describe the charts shown in the dashboard; they do not establish the causes of the observed trends.
